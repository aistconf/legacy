Sponsors & Partners

The conference is organized by a consortium of nonprofit organizations, leading universities, research centres, institutes of the RAS, the scientific interests of which are tightly connected with the research and development of the mathematical models for data mining among the algorithms and tools. AIST is also supported by those companies that are engaged in the analysis of graphical, textual, and other structured and unstructured data.
