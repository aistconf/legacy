Analysis of Images, Social Networks, and Texts

The 4th international conference on Analysis of Images, Social Networks, and Texts

AIM AND SCOPE

AIST is a scientific conference on Analysis of Images, Social Networks, and Texts. The conference is intended for computer scientists and practitioners whose research interests involve Internet mathematics and other related fields of data science.

LIST OF TOPICS

  • Analysis of Images and Video
  • Natural Language Processing
  • Social Network Analysis
  • Recommender systems and collaborative technologies
  • Geoinformation systems
  • Game analytics
  • Core Data Mining and Machine Learning techniques
  • Semantic Web and Ontologies
  • Educational Data Mining
  • ML&DM for Economics and Social Sciences

ORGANIZERS, SPONSORS & PARTNERS

AIST conference in 2015 was organized by Ural Federal University, Krasovsky Institute of Mathematics and Mechanics UB RAS, and nonprofit organization Graphicon with support from Exactpro Systems LLC, IT centre, SKB Kontur, JetBrains and Yandex.

The complete list of our sponsors and partners is provided at the Sponsors & Partners page.

LOCATION

The fourth conference on Analysis of Images, Social Networks, and Texts was held at the Palladium business centre, Yekaterinburg, Russia from Thursday, 9th through Saturday, 11th of April 2015.
