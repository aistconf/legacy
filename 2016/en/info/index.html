Extra information for participants

Venue

The conference will take place at Palladium Business Center at Khohryakova street 10, Yekaterinburg, Russia (map) from Thursday, 7th through Saturday, 9th of April 2016.

Please use Booking, Ostrovok or other similar services to search through Ekaterinburg hotels close to Khohryakova street 10.

Schedule

AIST conference starts on Thursday, April 7 at 9:00 in the morning and will be divided into scientific sections that will be held on Thursday and Friday and business sections that will be held on Saturday.

The authors with the plenary talks this year will have 60 minutes including questions. All the authors of full sectional papers that will be published at the main volume of the Conference proceedings will have 20 minutes including questions. Those with papers at the supplementary volume and short papers at the main volume of the Conference proceedings will have 10 minutes including questions.

The schedule of the scientific sections can be found here.
The schedule of the business sections is available at dataconf.org (AIST business day website).

Three Slides Marathon

The Three Slides Marathon will be held onFriday, April 8 in the evening. Each participant of the marathon is asked to prepare a short 2-minute presentation with three slides or so and A1 vertical printed poster to communicate his or her research goals, ideas and results.

Please, send your slides in advance to org@aistconf.org.

Language & Slides

Official languages of the conference are English & Russian. The choice of the language of presenting is up to the speakers.
But we kindly ask you to prepare all slides in English in 4*3 format.

We plan to accompany each foreign participant with a personal interpreter.

Proceedings

We follow the post proceedings publication model. Therefore, we will publish only those papers which are presented at the conference. Each author of proceedings will receive a special token from Springer Publisher, which allows free access to the papers.

Similarly, the supplementary volume of the proceedings (at CEUR) will be available soon after the conference.
