GOLDEN SPONSORS

Exactpro, LLC specialises in quality assurance for exchanges, investment banks, brokers and other financial sector organisations worldwide. Founded in 2009, Exactpro operates in the UK and US, with significant functions in Russia. It currently employs almost 300 people.

The consulting practices team closely follows current trends in the securities trading industry and carries out research in the areas of systems quality, performance and reliability. Exactpro Systems is also working on productizing trading support and monitoring techniques in the form of a suite of scalable event processing products.

As a fully owned subsidiary of London Stock Exchange Group (LSEG), Exactpro is part of LSEG's Technology Services division, which also incorporates MillenniumIT and GATElab.

Odnoklassniki is the most famous Russian social network according to FOM with monthly audience of 47,3 million people (LiveInternet.ru, December 2015). Registration on the site is free. Odnoklassniki is a three-time winner of the Runet awards, a winner of the "People's Award", a winner of the Russian Entertainment Awards, the Grand Prix winner in the Master of Brand Building and the Grand Prix winner for "The impact on offline" in the professional competition ROTOR.

OK.ru is owned by Mail.Ru Group (LSE:MAIL, listed since November 5, 2010), leading Internet company in Europe and is the 1st largest Internet business globally, based on the average minutes per visitor (comScore, top 100 properties, December 2015, worldwide).

BRONZE SPONSORS

«Centre of Information Technologies» (Centre IT) - Russian engineering company that specializes in creating exclusive IT solutions and services for the interdepartmental electronic interaction on the national and international levels.

Company focuses on development of effective global information systems processing millions of information units and uniting an unlimited number of users. Centre IT creates comfortable, intelligible and secure IT products for governments, business and people.

The structure of Centre IT comprises the head commercial office in Moscow, technological offices in Yekaterinburg and Chelyabinsk, and developer teams in other Russian cities. Today Centre IT employs over 130 specialists.
