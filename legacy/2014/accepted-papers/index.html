Analysis of Images, Social Networks, and Texts

Accepted Papers

We have received a lot of very high quality submissions, and all submissions were reviewed by at least three experts in the field. Out of the 74 submissions we have received, the program committee have selected 25 papers for publication in the main volume, and 44 papers and posters for publication in the supplementary volume. Withdrawn papers are not listed here.
