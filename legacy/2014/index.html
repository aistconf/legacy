Analysis of Images, Social Networks, and Texts

The 3rd conference on Analysis of Images, Social Networks, and Texts

AIM AND SCOPE

AIST is an international data science conference on Analysis of Images, Social Networks, and Texts.
The conference is intended for computer scientists and practitioners whose research interests involve Internet mathematics and other related fields of data science.

AIST'2014 was held on 10-12 April at Hyatt Regency Ekaterinburg Hotel (8, Borisa Yeltsina Street, Yekaterinburg, Russia).

PROGRAM

AIST'2014 conference program included four types of presentations

  • Plenary lectures and tutorials by leading international scientists
  • Industry presentations
  • Contributed talks selected by the program committe
  • Poster session
